Metadata, taxonomy, placeholders and spreadsheets

This sample page uses all four. The line below the title comes from page metadata (author, publication date, reading time). The tags are taxonomy tag IDs shown with readable titles. Their labels ("Written by", "Topics") come from the placeholders spreadsheet. The office table further down is a spreadsheet rendered by the Data Table block.

Page metadata
